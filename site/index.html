Two ways to prove your skills

Broad role capability meets focused practice.

Certifications and Applied Skills are complementary Microsoft credentials. Use both to tell a stronger skills story.

C

Microsoft Certification

Show you can perform across a role.

  • Broad coverage across several skill areas
  • Aligned to roles such as administrator or architect
  • Earned by passing a Certification exam
  • Associate and expert credentials require renewal

Best for: role readiness, career progression, and broad technical credibility.

+
A

Microsoft Applied Skills

Show you can solve a focused scenario.

  • Narrow validation of one specific skill set
  • Aligned to a product or business challenge
  • Earned through a performance-based lab
  • Useful before, beside, or after a Certification

Best for: project readiness, hands-on evidence, and targeted upskilling.
